---
description: Builds or records Arketum's batch of pending decisions
---

Call Arketum's `build` tool with `root` (this project's folder, absolute path) and `action: batch`. Tell the person to answer in `.arketum/pending-decisions.md` (`decisiones-pendientes.md` in a store from an earlier format) or in the panel and, when they say they answered, call `action: batch` again to record it.
